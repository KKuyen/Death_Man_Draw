# ART_RESULT (delivery-agent, 04/10/2026)

Software GL only (headless Chromium + SwiftShader, ~1-4 fps). **FPS on a real GPU is unmeasured.** Animation timing was judged from time-scaled captures (`fx.timeScale`), not real-time playback. Audio was never listened to (procedural WebAudio code only, compiled and called, not heard).

## 1. "Không xem được bài trên bàn" (fixed)
Cause: community cards were 0.1x0.14 m flat on the felt, ordered along world X, dark lit textures, seen at a grazing angle. Now (apps/web/src/fx.ts): community cards 1.8x, standing at ~50 degrees leaning toward the local viewer, laid out along the viewer's own right axis (reads left-to-right from every seat), unlit 512x768 textures with big corner indices and centre pip. Showdown / forced-reveal cards fly to a reading spot in front of their owner, facing you, scaled by distance. Your hole cards: unchanged first-person hands (look-down), verified A-spade K-heart readable.
Verified by screenshots from all four seats (`apps/web/qa-art.mjs` -> `reports/screenshots/art-final/seatN-{board,hand,showdown}.png`; before: `art-before/`). I looked at seats 0-3 board and seats 0/2/3 hand/showdown. Not checked: 16:10/narrow aspect ratios, the cards under the DOM action bar, spectators (viewer fixed to the lobby camera direction).

## 2. Magic card art (scripts/cards)
Research and sources: `assets/MAGIC_CARD_STYLE.md`. Generator `node scripts/cards/gen.mjs` (catalog from packages/content/src/index.ts, MD fallback) renders 22 faces (N01-N09, K01-K10, X01-X03 as of the last catalog read), `plain/` variants without the visibility glyph, one back, 6 modifier overlays + 6 badges, `manifest.json`. All art is original vector; contact sheets: `reports/screenshots/cards-sheet-1.png`, `-2.png`, `-overlays.png` (reviewed: first full set; after the last catalog changes (K07 crystal ball, K10, N09) I inspected the generated K07/K10 only through the scene screenshots, not a fresh contact sheet). Three frame kinds (Kích hoạt red, Nội tại xuyên suốt violet, Nội tại kích hoạt teal), visibility glyph (public/hinted/hidden), price slot (base price baked).
Caveats: illustration quality is "clean vector cartoon", not hand-painted; effect text is tiny by request and is not readable in 3D at table distance (the DOM carries it). Fonts depend on the system serif (Georgia fallback); Vietnamese diacritics render but kerning was only eyeballed. K10 and N09 rendered from the catalog as of the last run (all 22 ids are in `manifest.json`); K10 vs K01 are twin eye motifs (magic-card iris vs playing card) and were not compared side by side in a fresh sheet.

## 3. Scene
Removed: fingers, tricks, marks, observed backs, held-up opposing cards, shop, gaze lean, fumble, mirror/lighter/etc. prop loading (only the deck prop remains). Added (all in `SCENE_API.md`): magic tray with buy-fly / use-burn, `showMagicUse` head pop-up (plain art + name tag, clamped into view), swap exchange in the hand, `peekFlash` (+crystal-ball variant), `showMagicPeek`, `showMagicHint` (edge pulse + text), `pulseSlot` for triggered passives, forced-reveal and board-replace animations, modifier overlays on cards, embers particle system, dealer shuffle (`shuffleDeck`, automatic before every deal), `magicSound.ts` (6 families + hint + shuffle).
Verified in `qa-magic.mjs` captures (`reports/screenshots/magic/`, `magic2/`): buy fly, tray, popups (seat 1 and 2, own), swap, peek card, hint line, pulse (sent, not visually inspected), use burn, forced reveal, crystal ball, shuffle frames (the shuffling stack is small from the seats; visible but subtle). Not verified: board-replace (K03) frame, `showMagicPeek` visually, `pulseSlot` visually, popups at seats 0/3 (edge clamp), multiple simultaneous popups overlapping, UI wiring of `magicHint`/`magicUsed` (UI agent), real server event flow, memory/GC of per-event allocations (popups create/dispose a mesh + texture each time).
`npx tsc --noEmit`: no errors in my files (one unrelated error in apps/web/src/ui/common.tsx at last run); `npm run build -w @saloon/web` passed.

## Honest limits
- Triggered-passive pulse needs either a private `use` event with the card's magicId or a UI call to `scene.pulseSlot`; I do not know which the engine emits.
- `peek` -> crystal ball is chosen by regex on `PeekEntry.label` (/cầu|crystal|ball/); `showMagicPeek` needs the UI to know the opponent's card id.
- QA harness (`apps/web/qa-scene.html`, `qa-vite.config.mjs`, `qa-art.mjs`, `qa-magic.mjs`) is dev-only; screenshots are regenerated, not committed contracts.
