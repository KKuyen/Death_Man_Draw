# Environment backdrop (Blender) — result, 04/10/2026

## Change
`scripts/blender/generate.py` (`backdrop()` called from `environment()`) adds geometry to the `env_saloon` mesh on the side opposite the bar, which was the open side that showed a flat dark void from seats 1/2:
- **Front wall** (Blender y=-3.95 = glTF z=+3.95, 4.5 m high): wainscot boards, posts, rails, crown beams, two windows with shutters, curtains and sills (warm "sunset" emissive panes), a framed desert painting (sun, mesas, cactus) with antlers, four wanted-poster frames, two bottle shelves, two wall sconces.
- **Left wall** (x=-4.95, 4.5 m): wainscot, posts, rails, crown beams, two windows, a red banner, a stack of barrels.
- **Wagon-wheel chandelier** (brass ring, spokes, six lamps) around the existing table-lamp chain at z=3.0.
- 4 new materials (sunset, canvas_sky, cactus, adobe). **No ceiling on purpose:** the web scene has a shadow-casting directional light above the room (`scene.ts` "window" light at y=7); a ceiling mesh would shade the whole room.
- Unchanged: all node names (none removed or added; same 18 nodes), all 16 anchors and their coordinates, table/seat/dealer positions, `env_saloon_sign`.
